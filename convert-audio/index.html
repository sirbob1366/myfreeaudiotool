This converter uses FFmpeg compiled to WebAssembly, running directly in your browser. The first time you convert, it downloads the converter engine (~30 MB, one time only — it's cached afterwards). After that, conversions start instantly and your files never leave your device.

Supported conversions

Convert any common audio format to MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG, FLAC or AAC — including MP3 to WAV, M4A to MP3, FLAC to MP3, OGG to MP3, WAV to FLAC, and dozens more combinations. Video files (MP4, WebM) can also be loaded to extract their audio track.

Which format should I pick?

  • MP3 — plays everywhere; the safe default for sharing.
  • WAV — uncompressed and lossless; best for editing, but large.
  • M4A (AAC) — better quality than MP3 at the same bitrate; native on Apple devices.
  • FLAC — lossless like WAV but roughly half the size.
  • OGG — open-source Vorbis; great for games and the web.

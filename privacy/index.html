Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 10, 2026

The short version

Your audio files are never uploaded to us. Every tool on MyFreeAudioTool processes your files locally, inside your own browser, using the Web Audio API and WebAssembly. We have no server that receives, stores or inspects your audio. When you close the tab, the data is gone.

Your audio files

When you load a file into any tool on this site, it is read directly into your browser's memory on your device. Processing (trimming, converting, compressing, merging, boosting, speed changing, recording) all happens on your device. The processed result is saved by your browser straight to your own storage. At no point is any audio data transmitted to MyFreeAudioTool or any third party.

The same applies to microphone recordings made with the voice recorder: audio is captured and held in your browser's memory only.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to understand aggregate site usage — which pages are visited, roughly where visitors come from, and what devices they use. Google Analytics uses cookies and collects standard technical information such as your IP address and browser type. It never has access to your audio files. You can block analytics with any standard ad/tracker blocker without affecting how the tools work.

Advertising

The site may display advertising to keep the tools free. Ad providers may use cookies to serve relevant ads. Ads have no access whatsoever to your files or recordings.

Local storage

We store one small preference in your browser's localStorage: your light/dark theme choice. That's it. It never leaves your browser.

Third-party CDN resources

Some tools load open-source processing libraries (FFmpeg.wasm, LAME MP3 encoder) from public CDNs the first time you use them. This is a standard file download of program code — none of your data is sent in the process.

Children's privacy

The site is a general-audience utility and does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If we change this policy we will update this page and its date. Material changes will be noted prominently.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Reach us via the contact page.

Your sound, as artwork

Podcasters share them as episode art, musicians print them on posters, couples frame the waveform of "I do." This tool renders any audio file into a clean, anti-aliased waveform PNG at up to 4K — drawn fresh at the target resolution (never upscaled), with full transparency support for layering into Canva, Figma or Photoshop.

Three styles

  • Bars — symmetric bars around the centerline. The classic podcast look.
  • Line — a filled envelope silhouette. Elegant for posters and minimal designs.
  • Mirror — tall bars above, faded reflection below. The streaming-player aesthetic.

Tips for great results

  • For social media, transparent background + white or brand-colored waveform layers over any photo.
  • Music with dynamics (quiet verses, loud chorus) makes more interesting shapes than heavily compressed tracks.
  • Trim to the most dramatic 30 seconds first — short clips render bolder, denser waveforms.
  • The credit toggle is off by default — your art stays yours. Flip it on if you'd like to give us a nod.

FAQ

What do people use waveform images for?

Podcast artwork, audiograms, release visuals, YouTube thumbnails, posters and merch, website headers, and social announcements.

Can I get a transparent background?

Yes — the PNG exports with full alpha, ready to layer over any design.

What resolution should I export?

1920×600 for social and YouTube, 2560×800 for banners, 3840×1200 for print. Always re-rendered at target size, never upscaled.

Is my audio uploaded?

No — analysis and rendering happen entirely in your browser.

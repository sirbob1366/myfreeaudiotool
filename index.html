Frequently asked questions

Are my audio files uploaded anywhere?

No. Every tool on this site runs entirely inside your browser using Web Audio and WebAssembly. Your files stay on your device from start to finish — we never see them, store them, or transmit them. That also makes processing faster, because there's no upload or download queue.

Is MyFreeAudioTool really free?

Yes — every tool is completely free with no signup, no watermarks, and no daily limits. The site is supported by ads, never by selling your data (we couldn't anyway: we never receive your files).

What audio formats are supported?

You can load MP3, WAV, M4A/AAC, OGG, FLAC and most other common formats. Exports are available in WAV and MP3 across the tools, and the converter handles MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG, FLAC and AAC in both directions.

Is there a file size limit?

There's no hard limit imposed by us — processing capacity depends on your device's memory. Typical songs and recordings (up to a few hundred MB) work fine on modern phones and computers.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. All tools work on iOS Safari, Android Chrome and every modern desktop browser. No app install needed.

Everything in one editor — without the upload

This is the full MyFreeAudioTool workflow on a single screen: a zoomable, sample-accurate waveform you can cut, rearrange and polish, with the same effect engines that power our standalone tools — reverb, echo, bass boost, speed, vocal removal, 8D orbit and spectral noise reduction — applied to a selection or the whole track, each with live preview and full undo. Other "free online audio editors" upload your file to a server, cap its size, and watermark or paywall the export. This one physically can't: there's no server in the loop. Your audio is decoded into your browser's memory and never leaves it.

A workflow that feels native

  • Select like a pro — drag on the waveform, grab either edge to adjust, double-click for everything, Esc to clear.
  • Navigate fast — scroll to zoom at the pointer, Shift+scroll to pan, 0 to fit the whole track.
  • Edit fearlessly — every destructive action lands on a multi-level undo stack (Ctrl+Z).
  • Effects that splice cleanly — apply reverb to just the last word of a phrase and the tail blends into the audio that follows, the way a real DAW renders it.
  • Continue from any tool — every effect page has an "Open in Editor" button that carries the processed audio straight here.

FAQ

Is this audio editor really free with no catch?

Yes — no signup, no watermarks, no export limits, no "pro" tier. It runs on your device, so there's no per-file server cost for us to recoup.

Are my files uploaded to a server?

No. Decoding, editing, effects and export all happen in your browser's memory. The editor keeps working offline after the page loads.

What can I do with the editor?

Cut/copy/paste/delete/crop/silence on a zoomable waveform; fades, normalize, reverse; and reverb, echo, bass boost, speed, vocal removal, 8D and noise reduction with preview — all with multi-level undo and WAV/MP3 export.

Does it work with large files and on mobile?

Yes — no fixed size limit (device memory is the ceiling), and the editor is touch-friendly: drag to select, zoom buttons, same edits everywhere.

Reverse any audio file in one step

Drop a file and it's reversed the instant it finishes decoding — the waveform you see is already the backwards version. Reversal is a pure sample operation (the audio data is rewritten back-to-front), so it's lossless, instant even for long files, and runs entirely in your browser with the Web Audio API.

What people use this for

  • Music production — reversed cymbal swells, reverse reverb tails and risers are studio staples. Reverse a crash, layer it before a drop, done.
  • Hidden messages & content — the classic "what does it say backwards?" format for shorts and reels.
  • Sound design — reversed speech and textures make instant sci-fi ambience.
  • Ear training — hearing familiar music backwards is a surprisingly good exercise in active listening.

FAQ

Does reversing lose any quality?

No — reversing just rewrites the samples in opposite order. Export as WAV for a bit-perfect reversed copy; MP3 re-encodes at 192 kbps.

Why would I reverse audio?

Reversed cymbals and risers are classic production effects, reversed speech is a popular content gimmick, and sound designers use reversal for textures constantly.

Can I reverse just part of a track?

Trim the section with Trim Audio first, reverse it here, then splice it back with Merge Audio.

Record audio online — no app needed

This recorder uses your browser's built-in MediaRecorder API. When you hit Start, your browser asks for microphone permission; the audio is captured and held in memory on your device only. We never receive a single byte of your recording.

Download formats

The native download is whatever your browser records best — typically WebM (Opus) on Chrome, Edge and Firefox, or MP4 (AAC) on Safari and iPhone. Need broader compatibility? Use Download as MP3 and the recording is converted right on your device.

Tips for better recordings

  • Use headphones to avoid speaker echo if you're monitoring.
  • Get within ~20 cm of the mic and record a 5-second test first.
  • Pair it with our audio trimmer to cut silence from the start and end, or the volume booster to normalize a quiet take.

Boost audio volume online

Quiet voice memo? Lecture recording you can barely hear? Drag the slider to amplify your audio up to 400% and listen to the change live before exporting. Boosting happens with the Web Audio API directly in your browser, so there's no upload, no queue and no quality loss beyond the boost itself.

What does Normalize do?

Normalize scans your whole file for its loudest peak, then raises (or lowers) the entire track so that peak sits at −1 dB — the loudest level that's safely below clipping. It's the one-click way to make a track as loud as possible without distortion.

Watch out for clipping

If you boost past the point where peaks exceed 100%, the loudest moments will distort. The slider warns you when your current setting will clip — use Normalize if you want maximum clean volume.

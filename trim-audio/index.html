How to trim an audio file online

  1. Load your file — drop any MP3, WAV, M4A, OGG or FLAC file onto the page.
  2. Select the part to keep — drag the coral handles on the waveform to mark the start and end of your clip.
  3. Preview — hit Preview to hear exactly what you selected.
  4. Download — export your trimmed clip as WAV (lossless) or MP3 (smaller).

Why this trimmer is faster

Most online audio cutters upload your file to a server, process it there, and make you download the result. This one decodes and cuts the audio directly in your browser with the Web Audio API — there's no upload time at all, and your file stays completely private on your device.

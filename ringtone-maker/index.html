How to add a ringtone to iPhone

  1. Export your clip as M4A and save it to your computer.
  2. Rename the file extension from .m4a to .m4r (that's Apple's ringtone extension — same format, different name).
  3. Connect your iPhone and open Finder (macOS Catalina or later) or iTunes (Windows / older macOS).
  4. Drag the .m4r file onto your device in the sidebar — it syncs into Settings → Sounds & Haptics → Ringtone.

How to add a ringtone to Android

Much simpler: export as MP3, copy it to your phone's Ringtones folder (or just download it directly on your phone), then pick it under Settings → Sound → Phone ringtone.
